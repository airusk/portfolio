It's easier than ever to make your own blog, at the same time it's less likely than ever for a human to read your blog.

This first post can provide a bit of context to the state of the world right now, at least from someone part of the dwindling middle class in the United States.

From where I'm standing, the world has yet to adopt AI in the avenues it has demonstrated value. It's built upon the work of humanity, but the way it reasons is unknowable, and its solutions, non-deterministic. Still, it works fast, it never clocks out, and it's only getting more reliable. I believe its adoption is inevitable, but I don't believe the fear comes from the unknown. I think the fear comes from the same problems that plague any gold rush; exploitation, economic divide, and a greater burden than we can calculate on our crucible called home. The fate of so many lies on the shoulders of so few. Time and time again we hope for the best, but expect the worst.

On a lighter note, interest in *Dungeon Crawler Carl* is at an all-time high, and people are once again flocking to the cinemas. Theater is saved once again! Television, Streaming, and COVID be **damned!**
